StayPlan Support

Last updated: 28 September 2026

StayPlan keeps your events on device after force-quit and cold reopen, with a backup you can see and export.

Common questions

  • Persistence: Add an event, force-quit the app, and reopen — your event should still be there. If it is not, email us with device model and iOS version.
  • Backup / export: Use Export to create a file you own (for example Plans.ics) via Share or Save to Files.
  • Account: No account is required for create, reopen, or export.
