StayPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

StayPlan (“the App”) is a local-first month planner for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we process

  • Events and plans you create stay on your device.
  • When you export or share a backup file (for example Plans.ics), that file is created on your device and shared only through the system share sheet you choose.
  • The App does not require an account. We do not operate our own servers for your events.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current App version.

Permissions

iOS may ask for access related to saving or sharing files you export. Those permissions are controlled by system Settings. We do not receive copies of your events on our servers.

Purchases

The App is a one-time paid App Store purchase. Apple processes payment. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: janisscholzapple@gmail.com or the support page linked from the App Store listing.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
